Archery Pop

Quick Game Instructions



Enter your name in the input field to start the game. The Play button will be enabled once you enter your name.



How to Play:

- Use your mouse to aim the scope (crosshair) inside the game area.

- Click falling targets to earn points.

- Targets will continuously fall from top to bottom.

- Avoid letting the targets reach the bottom of the game board.
